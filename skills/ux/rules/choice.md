# 21g UX · Choice and cognitive load

House default. When the project has its own patterns, express this through its components and flows (SKILL.md, rule zero).

## Laws
Full law, how to apply it and what to flag: `references/laws.md`.

- **A1** Hick's Law: few choices per step, a recommended default, the rest disclosed.
- **A4** Miller's Law: group and chunk; never make people remember things between steps.

## Field rules
Learned from real passes: `references/field-rules.md`.

- **F1** One control per job; remove duplicates.
- **F3** Secondary detail on demand (popover or detail view), nothing lost.
- **F6** Say each thing once.
- **F14** No controls that can't act here.
- **F17** Only offer real choices: one option means just do it.
- **F37** Reading and picking steps sit in a narrow centred column.
- **F40** Compact overview first, the full view on tap; "See all" expands in place.
- **F42** One- or two-word labels, the definition on request.
- **F46** One kind of thing per container; objects people act on are cards.
- **F57** The record leads its detail page; edit, delete and other actions sit on what they change.
- **F61** Times shown as a calendar; history summaries stay compact and open on tap.
