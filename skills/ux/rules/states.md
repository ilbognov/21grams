# 21g UX · States: empty, loading, error, success

House default. When the project has its own patterns, express this through its components and flows (SKILL.md, rule zero).

## Rules
Full rule, how to apply it and what to flag: `references/rules.md`.

- **#7** Never a blank screen while loading: skeleton, spinner or progress bar.
- **#10** Feeds load the next batch before people reach the bottom.

## Field rules
Learned from real passes and research: `references/field-rules.md`.

- **F34** Empty states that onboard.
- **F43** Missing data says N/A, never blank or zero.
- **F44** Line limits with an ellipsis; repeated slots aligned across siblings.
- **F45** Check none, one, typical, many and the longest values.
- **F51** Limits show used of total ("8/12"), countable and coloured by what is left.
- **F53** Every blocked state offers its fix on the same screen.
