# 21g UX · Flows and task length

House default. When the project has its own patterns, express this through its components and flows (SKILL.md, rule zero).

## Laws
Full law, how to apply it and what to flag: `references/laws.md`.

- **A6** Tesler's Law: the product carries the complexity with defaults, inference and templates; advanced control stays reachable.

## Field rules
Learned from real passes: `references/field-rules.md`.

- **F9** No version numbers, design notes or internal jargon on screen; one form of address.
- **F10** New UI stays inside the product's stated scope.
- **F13** No step that repeats the choice that opened it.
- **F35** Honest money: free path, dated trials, upgrades at the limit.
- **F47** Roles change the starting point, not the app.
- **F48** One picker for add and edit; an empty selection opens it.
- **F53** Every blocked state offers its fix on the same screen.
- **F54** Grace exceptions are a recorded choice the next person on shift sees.
- **F55** Actions that need approval are requests with a visible status.
- **F59** Only the states and concepts the business really has.
- **F64** Hardware steps are shown on screen and simulated in demos.
