# 21g UX · Accessibility and keyboard

House default. When the project has its own patterns, express this through its components and flows (SKILL.md, rule zero).

## Laws
Full law, how to apply it and what to flag: `references/laws.md`.

- **A2** Fitts's Law: the primary action is large and close; 44×44 touch targets; destructive actions kept apart.

## Field rules
Learned from real passes: `references/field-rules.md`.

- **F5** Never remove the only keyboard, touch or screen-reader path.
- **F20** Shortcuts are shown and don't fight the browser.
- **F37** Narrow reading width for text and lists.
- **F41** No information only on hover; touch has none.
- **F60** Horizontal scrollers fade at the edges and scroll the selected item into view.
- **F63** Typing never lags or resets because of background updates.
