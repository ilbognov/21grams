# 21g UX · Navigation and wayfinding

House default. When the project has its own patterns, express this through its components and flows (SKILL.md, rule zero).

## Laws
Full law, how to apply it and what to flag: `references/laws.md`.

- **A3** Jakob's Law: standard placement, icons and platform conventions.

## Rules
Full rule, how to apply it and what to flag: `references/rules.md`.

- **#6** An X closes a modal or flow; a left arrow goes back one screen.
- **#8** Coming back to a feed restores the exact scroll position.
- **#9** Tapping the active tab returns to the top of that tab.
- **#14** Quick actions open in a bottom sheet that swipes down to dismiss.
- **#15** Notifications and shared links open the exact content, not home.

## Field rules
Learned from real passes: `references/field-rules.md`.

- **F2** Screen actions grouped top right, primary in the corner, apart from selectors.
- **F7** A list's count sits in its heading, follows filters, full figure on hover.
- **F11** Account at the bottom of the nav with sign-out; notifications top right; both on mobile.
- **F16** Contextual toolbars follow the selection.
- **F22** Canvas tools ignore zoom.
- **F38** Pin the step's context and action while scrolling; nothing else is sticky.
- **F39** Filters sit beside the list and open as a bottom sheet with Apply.
- **F50** Same patterns, names and order on mobile and desktop.
- **F58** Feeds are pages of their own; creating is a top-right button that opens a dialog.
- **F60** Horizontal scrollers fade at the edges and scroll the selected item into view.
- **F62** Navigation over about seven items is grouped by job.
